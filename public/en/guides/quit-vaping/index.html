How to quit vaping

Vaping looks more harmless than cigarettes, but most vapes contain nicotine, sometimes a lot. And since you can vape anywhere with no smell, it's easy to vape all day without noticing.

Quitting is hard because it's both a habit and a nicotine addiction. These steps help you prepare and get through the first weeks.

1. Pick a day

Choose a day in the next week or two and tell a close friend. A set day makes the decision real.

2. Know what to expect

In the first days you may feel irritable, find it hard to focus, sleep badly and have strong cravings. It isn't pleasant, but the worst symptoms usually pass in less than two weeks. A single craving lasts a few minutes and fades.

3. Notice when it happens

On breaks, on the way somewhere, at the computer, with certain friends, when stressed. Those are the moments you need a plan for.

4. Decide in advance what you do instead

"When I want to vape, I ___". For example:

  • Take 10 deep breaths
  • Drink a glass of cold water
  • Chew gum
  • Walk for 5 minutes

Your hands and mouth are used to the vape, so give them something else to do.

5. Get rid of the device

The device, pods and charger go out of your bag, car and home. When the vape isn't in your pocket, it's harder to vape without thinking.

6. Don't do it alone

There's free help. In the US, smokefree.gov has a free quit-vaping program and text support. Elsewhere, ask your doctor or your national quitline.

7. Count your clean days

Every day without vaping is a win. And if you slip, don't throw it all away, get back to the plan from the next puff.

Frequently asked questions

Vaping is less harmful than smoking, so why quit?

Most vapes still contain nicotine, which is addictive, and the dependence is the hard part. And if what bothers you is vaping without control, that's reason enough to quit.

Is it better to quit at once or cut down?

Both can work. Some people lower the nicotine strength step by step before quitting. A quitline can help you choose.

What if all my friends vape?

Tell them in advance you're quitting, and for the first weeks step away a little from the moments you vape together.
